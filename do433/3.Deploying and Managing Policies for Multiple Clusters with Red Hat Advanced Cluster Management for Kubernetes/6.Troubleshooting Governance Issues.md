# 3.11 Troubleshooting Governance Issues

- Issue categories: **Definition** (bad syntax/params), **Placement** (wrong namespace/cluster/placement rules), **Conflict** (overlapping policies on same resource, unclear precedence), **Evaluation** (engine/infra issues, worse at scale), **Remediation** (fix fails, conflicts with other policies, or manual drift).
- **Tools**: RHACM console (policy status/placement/violations), `oc` CLI (`oc get policies`, `oc describe policy <name> -n <ns>` — shows placement, binding, policy set, and events), logs (`oc logs -n open-cluster-management` on `grc-policy-propagator-*` and `grc-policy-addon-controller-*` pods), `oc describe operator gatekeeper` (verify Gatekeeper health — no events = healthy), IDEs with YAML/syntax validation, plus `oc get events` and `oc describe managedcluster` for broader context.
- **Policy ordering/conflicts**: use `dependencies` (policy-level) and `extraDependencies` (template-level) in the Policy spec to force one policy/resource to reach `Compliant` before another proceeds (e.g., wait for the compliance operator before running a scan-binding policy).

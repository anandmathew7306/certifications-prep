# Key takeaway (4.6 Summary)

Observability = Prometheus (collect) + Thanos (aggregate/store globally) + Alertmanager (route alerts) + Grafana (visualize), all orchestrated through the `MultiClusterObservability` CR. It's opt-in (needs S3 storage + real CPU/memory), and everything customizable — metrics, alerts, dashboards, alert routing — goes through that CR or its companion ConfigMaps/Secrets in the `open-cluster-management-observability` namespace.

# Key takeaway (5.10 Summary)

GitOps (via OpenShift GitOps/Argo CD) drives declarative, auditable, consistent app delivery. RHACM adds the multicluster layer: `ApplicationSet` + `Placement` fan an app out across the fleet, in either Push (hub-driven) or Pull (cluster-driven) mode. Topology view (RHACM) and the Argo CD console are the primary troubleshooting tools, and most failures trace back to `ApplicationSet`/`Placement` misconfiguration or bad manifests in Git.
